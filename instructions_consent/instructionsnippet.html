In the trial and in all five consecutive parts, you will work on the task you just saw for at most eight minutes. You will receive a fixed payoff {{ if not player.treatment }} of {{ C.TRUE_PAYOFF }} points {{ endif }} for each correctly solved encryption task in the part that will be randomly chosen for payment. {{ if player.treatment }} Right now, you only know that the payoff is between {{ C.BENEFIT_RANGE_MIN }} and {{ C.BENEFIT_RANGE_MAX }} points per task. {{ else }} Besides working on the encryption task, you have an additional task to do: a number between {{ C.BENEFIT_RANGE_MIN }} and {{ C.BENEFIT_RANGE_MAX }} has been chosen, and you will have to guess this number. {{ endif }}

Each part of the study will have the following stages:


1. Guessing the {{ guess_about }}

Before the trial, and in all parts from two to five, you will have to state how large you think the {{ guess_about }} is based on your current information. You can earn a bonus payment of {{C.BELIEF_BONUS}} points if your guess in the randomly chosen part is close to the true {{ guess_about }}. The more precise your guess – i.e., the closer it is to the true {{ guess_about }} –, the more likely it is that you win {{C.BELIEF_BONUS}} points. Given this payment rule, you should state your best guess to ensure the largest chance of receiving the {{C.BELIEF_BONUS}} points. If you are interested in the exact mathematical formula stating the precise payment rule, please click on the button “Show mathematical formula”.


The probability of receiving the payment is

{{ if player.treatment }} $$ {p = 1- \frac{(\text{your guess} - \text{actual payoff per task})^2}{900}.} $$ {{ else }} $$ {p = 1- \frac{(\text{your guess} - \text{actual chosen number})^2}{900}.} $$ {{ endif }}

If your guess equals the actual {{ guess_about }}, then p=1, and you will get the extra payment for sure. The further your guess deviates from the actual {{ guess_about }}, the lower is the probability p that you receive the extra payment. If your guess deviates from the actual {{ guess_about }} by more than 30, then p=0, i.e., p can never turn negative.


2. Further information about the {{ guess_about }}

The {{ guess_about }} is the same in all parts of the study. It is the average of 60 numbers that lie between {{ C.BENEFIT_RANGE_MIN }} {{ if player.treatment }}points{{ endif }} and {{ C.BENEFIT_RANGE_MAX }}{{ if player.treatment }} points{{ endif }}. During the course of the study, you will get more and more information about the {{ guess_about }}, in the following way: At the beginning of parts two, three and four, we will briefly display 20 of these 60 numbers – represented by balls – on the screen. Each of the 60 balls will be displayed only once, so you will have seen all the balls after part four. See the examples of balls below.

Examples of numbers represented by balls


3. Working on the encryption task

In every part, you will have the opportunity to work on the encryption task for eight minutes. Your payment for the encryption task will be {{ if player.treatment }}the fixed payoff {{ else }} {{C.TRUE_PAYOFF }} points {{ endif }}times the number of tasks you completed correctly in the part randomly chosen for payment.

In the trial part, you have to work for the full eight minutes to become familiar with the task. However, in all other parts, you can stop working on the encryption task by clicking on the "Stop working" button at any point during the eight minutes. For the remainder of the eight minutes, we offer you to watch entertaining videos (see screenshots from example videos below). You will receive a flat fee of {{ C.FLAT_LEISURE_FEE }} points for every minute spent on watching the videos. Please note that once you decide to stop working, you will NOT be able to return to the encryption task in that part.

At the beginning of parts one to five, we will remind you about your performance in the previous part.


Screenshots from example videos


4. Questionnaire

Between each consecutive part, we will ask you to answer a few questions.



Payment for the study

Your final payoff will come from five sources:

  1. A fixed completion fee of {{ participation_fee }} if you complete the whole study.
  2. Payoff from your performance in a part randomly chosen by the computer (including the trial): number of tasks completed times {{ if player.treatment }}the true payoff per task{{ else }} {{C.TRUE_PAYOFF }} points{{ endif }}.
  3. {{ C.FLAT_LEISURE_FEE }} points per minute spent on watching videos in the same randomly chosen part.
  4. Bonus payment based on the accuracy of your guess about the {{ guess_about }} in another part randomly chosen by the computer (from the trial part and parts two to five).
  5. In one of the questionnaires between two parts, you will make a lottery decision. At the end, you will also get paid based on this decision.

All payments will be converted from points to USD at a rate of 1000 points = {{ thousand_points }}.



Further information

This study is conducted by researchers at the Düsseldorf Institute for Competition Economics at Heinrich Heine University Düsseldorf and financed by the German Research Foundation (DFG, SCHI 1377/2-1).

If you accept to participate in the study, you may still change your mind and quit at any time. However, please note that you only receive a payment if you complete the whole study.

Participation in this study is not associated with any foreseeable risk. Your answers will be collected confidentially and anonymously (the researchers will not be able to link decisions and participants' identity beyond the Prolific ID provided). At the data analysis stage, your Prolific ID will be changed to a random identifying number, and the Prolific IDs will be deleted. In case the results of the study are published, there can be no references to your identity. Data anonymity is guaranteed.

We ask you to not use any AI tools to help you complete the study. After the comprehension checks, you will be asked to confirm that you will not use any AI tools.

The study received ethical approval from the German Association for Experimental Economic Research e.V. (Certificate No. WtfUX4eq).
